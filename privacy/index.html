Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026.

Privacy-minded by design. Sprout House keeps plant-care information local where practical and only sends information to remote services when a feature needs it, such as sign-in, subscriptions, Sprig, image analysis, integrity protection, or support and safety reporting.

Information you create in Sprout House

You may create plant records, nicknames, species and care information, rooms and placement details, Plant Passport information, care and watering history, tasks, diary entries, light readings, photos, and preferences. Much of this information is stored on your device. A feature may also use remote processing or account-linked storage when this is necessary to provide that feature or when the app clearly tells you that remote processing is involved.

Accounts and identifiers

If you create or use a Sprout House account, the app may process information such as your name, user identifier, authentication state, and information needed to keep your account signed in and secure. Sprout House uses Firebase services for supported authentication and may use Firebase App Check or similar integrity controls to protect backend services. Authentication providers may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Photos, camera, and light features

Sprout House requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose a photo-related action. App-owned plant photos and optional room or light-map photos may be stored on your device. Removing an app-owned photo or resetting app data does not remove an original from your device photo library.

If you explicitly start plant photo analysis or light-map analysis and confirm the relevant disclosure, Sprout House may send the selected image and limited related context to its backend for that requested analysis. Sprout House does not automatically send your entire photo library.

Sprig and AI-powered features

When you choose a remote Sprig feature, Sprout House may send your question, limited conversation context, and relevant plant or room context to the Sprout House backend, which uses OpenAI to provide the requested response. Photo and light-image analysis are separate, user-triggered flows.

Responses API requests are configured with store: false. This setting concerns Responses API application-state storage; it is not a promise of zero provider retention, immediate deletion, or a statement about every provider practice.

If you report a Sprig response, the report may include the reported response, the preceding user message, your selected reason, any optional comment, and limited technical metadata needed to review the report. Reports are used for safety, moderation, quality, and service-improvement purposes.

Subscriptions and purchases

Sprout House may offer auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Apple processes App Store payment and billing information. Sprout House does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Sprout House may receive and process purchase history, product identifiers, transaction or subscription status, renewal or expiry state, and related entitlement information needed to provide paid features, restore purchases, prevent incorrect access, and support subscription changes. This information may be linked to your Sprout House user identifier when needed to provide subscription functionality.

Technical, diagnostic, and security information

Remote services may process technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as connection information, IP-derived rate-limiting state, request identifiers, integrity signals, and privacy-aware operational logs. Limited crash and performance information may also be made available through Apple or enabled service providers so we can diagnose failures, maintain reliability, and improve app functionality. Diagnostic information is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

How we use information and service providers

We use information to provide plant-care features, authenticate users, manage subscription entitlements, fulfill optional Sprig and image-analysis requests, review user-submitted reports, respond to support requests, and protect and improve service reliability. Depending on the feature, service providers may include Apple for App Store purchases and platform services, Firebase for supported authentication or integrity services, OpenAI for requested AI processing, and the infrastructure used to operate the Sprout House backend.

Retention, security, and international processing

Local information remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app-owned content, or reset app data. Account-linked, purchase, support, safety, and operational information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain entitlements and records, respond to requests, protect the service, or meet applicable legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the service, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable protections.

Your choices and deletion rights

You can remove app-owned data from relevant areas of Sprout House and use available account or reset controls for supported data. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of remotely processed information. See Delete your Sprout House account and data for the current deletion route, or contact hello@sprouthouse.app.

Children's privacy

Sprout House is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that would require parental consent under applicable law.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Sprout House changes. We will post the updated version here. For privacy or data requests, email hello@sprouthouse.app. For general help, visit Sprout House Support. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
