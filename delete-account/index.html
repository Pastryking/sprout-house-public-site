Delete your Sprout House account and data

Account and cloud data. Signed-in Sprout House accounts may have supported account data and eligible app-owned plant, growth, and Room Light Map media stored in account-scoped cloud services. Deleting only the local app data on one device does not necessarily delete cloud-restorable account data.

Request account deletion

To request deletion of your Sprout House account and associated cloud data, email:

hello@sprouthouse.app

Tell us that your request concerns deletion of your Sprout House account. Include only the information needed for us to identify and process the request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

What account deletion is intended to remove

The deletion process is intended to remove the Sprout House account identity and account-bound cloud data that Sprout House controls, including supported structured account data and eligible account-scoped Sprout House media where applicable. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or transaction-record purposes.

Remove local app data

You can remove individual plants and app-owned photos from their relevant areas, or use available reset controls in the app to clear supported local Sprout House data from that device. Resetting local data does not remove original photos from your device photo library. If you are signed in, local removal may not delete account-linked cloud data that can later be restored.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting a Sprout House account does not itself cancel an App Store subscription. Subscription billing and cancellation are managed through Apple. If you no longer want an active subscription, manage or cancel it through your Apple subscription settings separately.

Other privacy or data requests

If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of specific remotely processed information without deleting your entire account, email hello@sprouthouse.app and describe the request.
