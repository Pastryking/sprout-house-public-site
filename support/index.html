Support

Need help with Sprout House? Contact us and include enough detail for us to understand what happened.

Email support

hello@sprouthouse.app

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

What we can help with

  • Sign-in and account-access problems
  • Plant, room, care-task, photo, lighting, or Sprig issues
  • Subscription access, entitlement, restore-purchase, or plan-display problems
  • Bug reports and unexpected app behaviour
  • Privacy and data requests

Subscriptions and billing

Sprout House subscriptions offered on iPhone are purchased through Apple. Apple handles App Store payment processing and billing. For problems with access inside Sprout House after a purchase, restoration, renewal, downgrade, or cancellation, contact us at the support email above and do not include payment-card details.

Helpful information to include

When reporting an app problem, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, Sprout House app version, what you were trying to do, and any error message you saw. Screenshots are welcome when they do not contain information you do not want to share.
